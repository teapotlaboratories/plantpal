Method & caveats

How unit price was taken

Each line is priced at the distributor's lowest listed quantity break, which for many LCSC passives is a 100 -piece minimum. The line total is that unit price × the quantity the board needs, so the figure is the value of parts on one board — not the cash needed to buy them, which is higher because of minimum order quantities.

Not included

PCB fabrication, assembly, stencils, shipping, import duty, the LiPo cell, the display module on J4, the enclosure, and any attrition allowance. DigiKey's Coilcraft Marketplace listing also adds a $10 flat shipping fee that is excluded here.

U4 — AEM10941 estimate

Mouser blocks automated price retrieval, so the $6.73 used for the e-peas harvesting PMIC is converted from a Glyn Store list price of AU$9.50 at AUD/USD 0.7082. A direct Mouser quote is likely lower — confirm it before quoting this board.

D3 — substituted price

The specified ST(Semtech) LL4148 (LCSC C9808) is out of stock with no price shown. The $0.0139 shown is an equivalent in-stock LL4148 (C181117). This is a rounding error at board level but the specified part number needs re-sourcing.

Volume

Deeper breaks cut the passive and discrete cost materially, but the four cost drivers barely move — the solar cells fall to $2.19 each only at 500 +, and the ESP32 -C6 drops just 9% at its 5,000 -piece reel price. Expect the floor to stay near $30 a board.
